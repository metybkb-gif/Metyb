Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 19/09/2026 — This Privacy Policy explains how Metyb collects, uses, and protects your information.

1 Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Metyb. We respect your privacy. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

2 Information We Collect

  • Your email address (e.g., for login)
  • Your social media usernames:
    • Facebook username
    • Instagram username
    • YouTube channel/username
  • Payment-related information: Transaction ID, payment gateway details
  • Device-related information: Device model, OS version

3 Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect phone numbers
  • We do not collect passwords for Facebook, Instagram, or YouTube
  • We do not collect any information related to Telegram

4 Purpose of Data Usage

  • To provide boost services
  • To process your orders
  • To provide customer support
  • To improve the app

5 Payment and Charges Policy

  • All payments are processed via 100% safe and secure third-party gateways (e.g., Google Pay, QR, Paytm).
  • When you recharge, a 20% service charge is deducted from the total recharge amount.
  • The remaining 80% is added to your Metyb wallet.
  • You can use this 80% balance for boosts and other features within the app.

6 Refund Policy

  • If the payment is successful but the funds are not added to your wallet, and you provide valid proof (screenshot/Transaction ID), the full amount will be refunded.
  • If a boost or service is not delivered, the full amount for that order will be refunded to your wallet.
  • For refunds, contact: metyb.k.b@gmail.com
  • Refunds will be processed within 5–7 working days.

7 Data Sharing

  • We do not sell any of your personal information to third parties.
  • Information may be shared only for legal requirements or payment gateway purposes.

8 Data Security

  • We employ standard security measures to protect your data.
  • We never ask for or store your social media passwords.
